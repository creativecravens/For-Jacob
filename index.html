The story

Quiet on set

Aspiring filmmaker John has one goal: shoot his horror movie over break using the eerie, empty halls of Harrington University.

With most of the campus cleared out and a skeleton crew of friends willing to help, he finally has the perfect location, and just enough time.

But not everything behind the camera is what it seems.

Tensions flare. Old buildings creak in the dark. As the storm outside intensifies, so does the paranoia. The line between fiction and reality begins to blur, and John finds himself trapped in a nightmare of his own creation, where someone is playing a much deadlier role than he ever scripted.
